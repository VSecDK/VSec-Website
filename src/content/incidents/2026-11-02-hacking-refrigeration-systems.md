## Description

Five Danish businesses were targeted in attacks on cooling and freezing systems, according to reporting by Ritzau citing the Danish Agency for Societal Safety and Preparedness. The reported targets included a food producer, a catering company, and a biotechnology company; the other two businesses were not identified. The businesses reportedly contained the attacks quickly, with no major disruption reported. Similar attacks affected supermarkets elsewhere in Europe, but available reporting does not establish that Danish supermarkets were affected or that temperatures were changed at the Danish businesses. Coop and Salling Group said they had not been targeted.
