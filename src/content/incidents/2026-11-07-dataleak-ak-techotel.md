## Description

AK Techotel, provider of the Picasso hotel booking system, reported a leak of Danish hotel reservations on 7 November 2026. Hotels using the system reported that guests received fraudulent payment requests through WhatsApp and other messaging channels. News reports said unauthorized parties may have accessed guest names, contact details, and stay information. The leak's cause and point of origin were still under investigation; AK Techotel's managing director said it might have occurred elsewhere.
