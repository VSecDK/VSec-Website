## 🇩🇰 Denmark & Nordic

### Hidden Backdoor in Routers

A significant vulnerability, designated as CVE-2026-11405, has been discovered in certain routers, allowing hackers to gain full administrative access. This backdoor is attributed to an undocumented authentication mechanism in the 'login()' function, according to CERT. The impact of this vulnerability could be substantial, given the critical role routers play in network infrastructure. Organizations and individuals using affected routers are advised to take immediate action to patch or replace these devices to prevent potential breaches.
