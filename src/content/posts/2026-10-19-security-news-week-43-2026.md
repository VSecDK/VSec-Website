## 🇩🇰 Denmark & Nordic

### Denmark Data Breach

A significant data breach in Denmark has resulted in the exposure of personal information, including CPR numbers, names, and addresses, of about 8.8 million Danish citizens. The breach is attributed to the misuse of a company's legitimate access to the national register, which was protected by a weak password, "123456". This incident emphasizes the critical importance of strong password policies and the potential consequences of negligence in cybersecurity practices.

The breach was discovered after a delay, which further exacerbates the situation, allowing potential attackers more time to exploit the exposed data. It serves as a stark reminder of the vulnerabilities that exist in digital systems and the need for robust security measures to protect sensitive information.
