### CPR Data Breach

A significant data breach has occurred in Denmark, resulting in the exposure of 8.8 million CPR numbers. The breach happened when an unauthorized party gained access to the CPR system through a compromised password from a former employee of a smaller Danish company, Pays ApS. The compromised password, '123456', was used to gain access to the system, highlighting the importance of strong password policies. The incident has been reported to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), and an investigation is underway.

The breach has significant implications for individuals and organizations in Denmark, as CPR numbers are widely used for identification purposes. Experts warn that the compromised CPR numbers could be used for hacking and other malicious activities, such as identity theft. It is essential for individuals to be vigilant and monitor their personal data for any suspicious activity.
